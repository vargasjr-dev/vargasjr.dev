A tweet from Chamath laid out the modern economy as a chain of conversions, and I haven't stopped thinking about it since. Five layers, each one taking the output of the layer above it and turning it into something more useful:

```
natural resources
   │  Power
energy
   │  Compute
FLOPs
   │  Models
tokens
   │  Harnesses
free time
   │  Life
happiness
```

Follow anything valuable back down the chain and you eventually hit a natural resource. Follow sunlight forward and you end up at happiness. That's the whole game — each layer is an industry, and each one gets measured by how efficiently it feeds the next.

## Power: natural resources → energy

Everything starts here. The power layer converts oil, gas, uranium, sunlight, wind, and moving water into electricity at scale.

The major players: **ExxonMobil**, **Chevron**, and **Shell** still supply the bulk of the world's primary energy. **NextEra** and **Constellation** lead renewables and nuclear, with the nuclear side getting a second life from datacenter demand. **GE Vernova** and **Vestas** build the turbines, and **First Solar** builds the panels. Behind all of them sit the utilities that keep the grid from melting.

## Compute: energy → FLOPs

The compute layer converts electricity into floating-point operations. This is where energy becomes math.

The major players: **TSMC** fabricates the silicon, **NVIDIA** and **AMD** design it, and **Micron** and **SK Hynix** supply the memory that feeds it. On top of the chips, the hyperscalers — **Microsoft**, **Amazon**, **Google**, **Meta**, and **Oracle** — plus neoclouds like **CoreWeave** and **Crusoe** buy gigawatts' worth of the stuff and rent it out as clusters.

## Models: FLOPs → tokens

The models layer converts raw FLOPs into tokens — the universal unit of machine intelligence. A token is a fragment of a word; it's also, increasingly, a fragment of work.

The major players: **OpenAI**, **Anthropic**, **Google DeepMind**, **Meta**, **xAI**, **Mistral**, and **DeepSeek**. Each runs the same fundamental conversion — FLOPs in, tokens out — and competes on tokens per dollar, tokens per second, and how smart each token is.

## Harnesses: tokens → free time

The harness layer is the newest one, and in my view the most interesting: it converts tokens into completed work, and completed work into free time. Models produce intelligence; harnesses point that intelligence at your life — your inbox, your codebase, your calendar, your business.

The major players: **Claude** and **ChatGPT** as the consumer harnesses, **Cursor** and **GitHub Copilot** as coding harnesses, **Replit** and agent platforms like **Zapier** as automation harnesses — and our own **VargasJR**, which is a personal harness: it builds, maintains, and operates software so its principal doesn't have to.

This is also the layer that absorbs everything else. Health, construction, law — every profession is a pile of coordination and paperwork wrapped around a core of real expertise. Harnesses convert the pile into free time and leave the expertise.

## Life: free time → happiness

The final layer converts free time into the thing all the other layers are actually for. There's no single company to name here — the players are categories: **games**, **movies**, **music**, and **sports**. Everything people do with a free Saturday.

This layer is the oldest entertainment economy in the world, and it's about to become the biggest one.

## Where this goes

Here's the bet: within a few decades, once the top four layers are solved, the majority of the economy will live in the last one. Power, compute, models, and harnesses are all conversion problems, and conversion problems get cheap. Happiness doesn't.

When harnesses are good enough, health, construction, and law stop being industries you hire and start being services that hand you back your time. The economy shifts from "do this for me" to "enjoy yourself."
